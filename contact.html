Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding donations, field visits, and volunteering.

How can I volunteer with GSDF?

You can volunteer in field medical camps, teach rural children, help with digital media, or assist in winter relief drives. Send us a message or WhatsApp us directly to join our volunteer roster.

Can I donate in-kind (books, clothes, medical supplies)?

Yes! We accept new or gently used children's books, school bags, warm blankets, and sealed medical equipment at our registered Madhupur office.

Are donations eligible for 80G tax exemptions?

Yes, donations to Govind Social Development Foundation are eligible under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act. Request your receipt on our Donate page.

Can I visit the villages and see the impact?

Absolutely! We encourage transparency. Inform our team at least one week in advance, and our field coordinators will facilitate a visit to our learning centers and rural projects in Deoghar.

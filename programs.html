Action on the Ground

Strategic Initiatives Transforming Rural Lives

Structured, metrics-driven humanitarian programs designed to break the cycle of poverty and empower families across Jharkhand.

Initiative #1

Shiksha Setu (Bridge of Education)

Quality education is the single most powerful equalizer. In rural Deoghar, many children lack even the most basic learning essentials: notebooks, pencils, school bags, and winter shoes.

  • Distribution of comprehensive school starter kits (notebooks, stationery, bags).
  • Free evening remedial tutoring for first-generation school attendees.
  • Girl child scholarship and anti-dropout mentoring for village parents.
Sponsor School Kit (₹500) 5,000+ Students Enrolled
Initiative #2

Arogya Setu (Rural Mobile Health Camps)

Remote hamlets in Santhal Pargana often lie miles away from functional public health centers. GSDF brings doctors and critical medicines straight to their village doorsteps.

  • Maternal health checkups, prenatal guidance, and infant wellness screening.
  • Free vital prescription medicines, vitamin supplements, and iron tonics.
  • Mobile eye testing camps and cataract detection for rural senior citizens.
Support a Medical Camp (₹1,200) 10,000+ Patients Treated
Initiative #3

Jeevan Sahara (Nutrition & Winter Relief)

Winter temperatures in rural Jharkhand drop drastically, putting elderly villagers and young children at risk of hypothermia and severe respiratory illnesses without warm bedding.

  • High-grade thermal blanket distribution to homeless and destitute elders.
  • Child nutrition packets (poha, protein biscuits, dry rations) for tribal villages.
  • Rapid flood and seasonal calamity food relief kits.
Donate a Relief Kit 15+ Relief Drives Conducted

Nari Samriddhi (Women Empowerment)

Equipping rural women with vocational sewing machines, tailoring certifications, and establishing self-help micro-enterprises to generate independent household income.

  • Tailoring & handicraft skill training
  • Menstrual hygiene & sanitation workshops
  • Micro-savings and bank account linkage

Harit Bharat (Clean Water & Trees)

Afforestation drives across degraded village commons, planting fruit-bearing native trees, creating rainwater recharge pits, and testing village well water safety.

  • Over 10,000+ saplings planted
  • School water filter installations
  • Anti-plastic & green village awareness

Our Methodology

How We Ensure Maximum Impact

A structured 4-step execution framework ensuring zero wastage.

01

Ground Assessment

Direct surveys with village pradhans and mothers to identify specific urgent needs.

02

Resource Mobilization

Procuring books, medicines, and food rations in bulk at direct ethical non-profit rates.

03

Field Execution

Deploying on-ground teams and medical volunteers directly into the villages.

04

Transparent Reporting

Publishing photo verification, beneficiary counters, and audited accounts publicly.

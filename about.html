Our Roots & Philosophy

Bridging Hope & Opportunity in Grassroots India

Born in the rural heart of Jharkhand, Govind Social Development Foundation exists to ensure no child is deprived of education, no family lacks basic healthcare, and every woman has the tools for self-reliance.

Our Genesis

From a Local Spark to a Regional Movement

Govind Social Development Foundation was established with headquarters in Sirsa via Madhupur, in the Deoghar district of Jharkhand. Witnessing the severe hardships faced by tribal and marginalized farming communities—where children dropped out of school to support family subsistence and basic medical attention was hours away—our founders united to build an organized, sustainable vehicle for long-term change.

What started as an informal collective providing emergency winter blankets, dry ration packets, and free tutoring classes under banyan trees has grown into an officially registered non-profit organization touching over 300 villages.

Guiding Principles

Vision, Mission & Core Values

The immutable ethical framework that guides every decision, camp, and program we undertake.

Our Vision

A self-reliant, educated, and equitable India where rural citizens have dignified access to premier learning, preventive healthcare, livelihood security, and a healthy natural ecosystem.

Our Mission

To empower marginalized and tribal families across Jharkhand through community-driven educational interventions, maternal and child healthcare, vocational training for rural women, and afforestation drives.

Our Core Values

Uncompromising Transparency: Every contribution is tracked.
Grassroots Dignity: We work shoulder-to-shoulder with villagers.
Zero Discrimination: Unconditional service to every human in need.

Growth & Milestones

Our Road to Impact

A decade of persistent groundwork, building trust village by village.

2021

Official Foundation & Registration

Formalized Govind Social Development Foundation as a registered body to expand organized relief, legal compliance, and community outreach.

2022

Rural Health Camps Launch

Mobilized volunteer doctors and health workers to conduct free mobile medical and eye testing camps across Deoghar's tribal belt.

2023 - 2024

Shiksha Setu & Women SHGs

Distributed study kits, bags, and books to over 5,000 children and initiated vocational tailoring self-help groups for village women.

2025 - 2026

Regional Expansion & Modern Vision

Scaling to 300+ villages, integrating digital learning kiosks, clean water borewells, and massive annual winter warmth drives.
